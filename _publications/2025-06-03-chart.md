**Abstract:** We investigate the chart understanding ability of vision-language models (VLMs), where the VLM is asked to answer questions about information presented in a chart. Our analysis identifies a perception bottleneck: while VLMs excel at textual reasoning, they struggle to accurately parse visual information from charts. We show that this bottleneck can be alleviated through chart-specific training, and that fine-tuning on a diverse set of chart types significantly improves performance across benchmarks. Our results suggest that chart understanding is primarily a visual parsing challenge rather than a reasoning challenge for current VLMs.
