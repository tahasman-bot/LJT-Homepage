**Abstract:** We propose a method for composing parameter-efficient modules (e.g., LoRA adapters) using arithmetic operations. We show that modules trained on different tasks can be combined through addition and multiplication to create new modules for unseen tasks, without any additional training. Our method works by treating modules as vectors in a low-dimensional space and composing them using operation-inspired combinations. Experiments across GLUE and SuperGLUE show that our compositional approach outperforms finetuning from scratch when target task data is limited, and approaches fully finetuned performance with sufficient training examples. This work demonstrates the promise of modular composition as a paradigm for scalable, task-general model adaptation.
