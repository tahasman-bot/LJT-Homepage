**Abstract:** We present C-Eval, a comprehensive evaluation suite for Chinese foundation models covering 52 subjects across 4 levels of difficulty and 4 disciplines. C-Eval includes 13,948 multiple-choice questions, covering humanities, social sciences, STEM, and other topics, with questions at high school, college, graduate, and professional levels. We evaluate 16 foundation models on C-Eval, providing a detailed analysis of model capabilities across subjects. Our results show that current models still struggle with more advanced subjects and that model performance varies significantly across disciplines. C-Eval serves as a standardized benchmark for assessing Chinese language model capabilities and identifying areas for improvement.
